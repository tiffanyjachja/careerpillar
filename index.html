Ladders tell you how to climb. Not who to become.

careerpillar starts from the person, not the rung. Log the work you actually did, see where it already points, pick a direction on purpose, and walk into your review with receipts.

stage one · egg

Log it while it's warm

One conversation per accomplishment: what you did, who it helped, the number if there is one, and the links that prove it. Months behind? Backfill from your merged pull requests.

/careerpillar:log/careerpillar:backfill
stage two · caterpillar

Eat everything, map everything

Every entry lands on a competency matrix. Coverage shows up as strong, thin, or gap against your target level, so the fuzzy feeling of "am I ready?" turns into a list.

/careerpillar:status/careerpillar:suggest/careerpillar:vibe-check
stage three · chrysalis

Find your shape

Senior is a destination, not a waypoint. Three explorers: the shape of the work you want, the two values you would defend when they cost you something, and the five ways you work at your best. Each ends in a hypothesis, an ask for your manager, and 90-day experiments. Staying senior and moving into management both count as answers.

/careerpillar:explore/careerpillar:values/careerpillar:strengths
stage four · butterfly

Make the case

Promotion packet, performance review, or self-review, generated from your evidence and your company's own template. Managers get a readiness check, a 360 draft, and a plan for peer feedback.

/careerpillar:export/careerpillar:manager-review/careerpillar:peer-review/careerpillar:peer-feedback

Three ways to find your shape

Ladders describe populations. These describe you. Each one ends in something you can say to your manager.

🧭

Shapes

Four staff archetypes plus the honest alternatives: stay senior, go manage, build breadth. Your log is scored against each, so the read starts from what you actually did.

/careerpillar:explore
💎

Values

Thirty-five cards. Everything that resonates, then ten, then five, then the two you would defend when they cost you something. Plus what you will not tolerate, which is usually the faster way in.

/careerpillar:values
⚡

Strengths

Twenty plain-language working patterns in four families. Pick five, see each at its best and running hot, and learn what to ask for. Written for this project, not a commercial assessment.

House rules

The parts that don't change, whatever your company's ladder looks like.

🔒 Your data stays home

One JSON file in your home directory. The dashboard uses your browser's storage. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

🧩 Bring your own ladder

Drop in your company's matrix, packet template, and role names as private files. The defaults are generic on purpose.

🧾 Receipts or it didn't happen

Every entry wants a real date, a stakeholder, a number or an honest "none", and a link. That's what survives a promo committee.

🌼 Senior is a real destination

Staying senior, going staff, or moving into management are all legitimate answers. The explorer treats them that way.
